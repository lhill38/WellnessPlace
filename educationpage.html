What Just Happened?

Training Guidelines: How to Spot a Phish

  • **Check the Sender's Email:** Is the address slightly wrong? (e.g., `wellnesplace@gmail.com` instead of `support@wellnessplaceaustralia.com.au`).
  • **Inspect the Link/URL:** Before clicking, **hover your mouse** over the link. Does the domain name look suspicious or incorrect? (e.g., `wellnessplace.com-sign-in.net`).
  • **Look for Urgency/Threats:** Phishing emails often use high-pressure language ("Account Suspended," "Immediate Action Required") to bypass critical thinking. Real companies rarely demand immediate, unverified action.
  • **Report Suspicious Emails:** If an email feels off, **do not click anything.** Instead, forward it to our IT Security Team (e.g., `security@yourcompany.com`) or use your organisation's **"Report Phishing" button**.
